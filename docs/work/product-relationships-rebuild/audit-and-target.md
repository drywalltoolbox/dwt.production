They cannot safely share one undifferentiated edge type or one bidirectional rule.
3. **Conflict review is read-only and bounded (high; partially resolved).** The workbench now scans relationship inputs and reports duplicate identities, missing/unpublished/variation targets, malformed lists, overlaps, and legacy links with downloadable evidence. It does not adjudicate catalog fitment or persist reviewer decisions; those remain in digest-bound review manifests.
4. **Merchandising mutation safeguards (resolved in current source; live source parity verified).** WooCommerce CRUD saves enforce target validity, duplicate and cross-type exclusion, optimistic revision, serialization by product lock, and post-write read-back. Fitment mutation remains outside the UI.

Live source/runtime parity is reconciled, but 383 published parts have no structured fitment and the evidence supporting existing and proposed fitment has not been fully adjudicated.
6. **Protected identity exposure (relationship surface resolved; adjacent pathway guarded).** Product Relationships has no identity editing and does not infer identity from display labels. Parts Manager's save and CSV import paths reject changes to an existing product's SKU or manufacturer part number, reject conflicting SKU ownership, and use WooCommerce product CRUD for SKU persistence. This code-level evidence does not replace a production request-path proof.
7. **Runtime/source parity (catalog/product fields reconciled; operator rendering/release still open).** All 821 WooCommerce products and 312 variations were identity-matched to canonical rows, including type and variation parent SKU. Fitment fields match across every product and variation, with zero unresolved target identities. Two variation publication flags differ as described above. 8. **Universal seed path/runtime availability (partially corrected; runtime unverified).** The deployed resolver supports the production artifact directory and canonical checkout directory, and both operator read paths now share it. Verify the actual production seed summary and all three readable CSVs through the owning runtime operation before relying on counts or planning projection writes.

## Architecture decision for the rebuild

Keep the current domain owners and persistence mechanisms; do not add a generic relationship table or a new relationship module.

- `products/` remains the canonical authority for compatibility. Exact schematic proposals are converted into a catalog/row-digest-bound manifest with evidence references and `needs_review` status. - WooCommerce `_dtb_compatible_tool_skus` and `_dtb_replacement_part_for` values are runtime projections. The wp-admin relationship workspace reads them together, labels their source, and exposes numeric legacy ID links as unverified. It does not directly publish fitment claims or convert legacy IDs.
- WooCommerce upsell/cross-sell edges remain stored through WooCommerce product CRUD. They are directional merchandising and stay separate from fitment.
- Schematic occurrence/mapping lifecycle remains in `dtb-schematics`; the relationship workspace links to its owner but does not edit schematic records.
- Universal-part identity/equivalence remains in `products/launch/universal_parts/*.csv` with Woo product metadata as its projection. The workspace may link to Parts Manager for sync/readiness; it does not rewrite seed identity or activate review/quarantine rows.
- The admin workspace is one operator surface over owner-specific adapters. Typed candidates and conflict results are read models; they do not become a second catalog authority.

The design removes the variable-product editor and direct fitment mutation from the relationship surface, and hardens the separate parts save/import path against identifier changes. WooCommerce CRUD remains the writer for native upsell/cross-sell. No checkout, product identity, schematic ownership, universal-part identity, inventory, or commerce-persistence authority moves.
