# DTB business, customer jobs, and evidence boundaries

**Last curated:** 2026-10-30. This is a marketing reference, not a replacement for the repository engineering contract or live business systems.

## Business frame

Drywall Toolbox serves professional drywall contractors and crews through specialist ecommerce for drywall taping and finishing tools, replacement parts, compatible-part discovery, schematics, repair services, returns, support, and fulfillment. This business description is grounded in the repository operating contract; it does not establish current availability of any particular service or product.

## Customer jobs to investigate

Common tasks supported by the product and service domains include choosing a tool system for a job, identifying an existing tool/model or generation, locating a schematic or maintenance instruction, confirming an exact part, comparing repair against replacement, finding a qualified repair path, and purchasing equipment or consumables. Treat these as useful workflow hypotheses until validated through customer interviews, support records, and behavioral data.

## Evidence labels

Use clear labels for verified source fact, dated observation, user-reported information, company/brand claim, calculation, inference, hypothesis, recommendation, and unknown. Record source, date, scope, and material limitations. A page or product listing proves only the content visible on that page at the retrieval time. Do not infer sales, market share, demand, performance, customer preference, stock, or checkout readiness from category existence, page views, search results, or broad market statistics.

## Authority boundaries

- Canonical product identities and source catalog inputs belong to `products/`; protected identifiers include SKU, MPN, GTIN, brand, taxonomy, and compatibility IDs.
- WooCommerce owns runtime products, cart, checkout, orders, payment state, refunds, customers, and commerce persistence.
- Veeqo owns inventory, allocation, fulfillment, shipping, shipment status, and tracking.
- Manufacturer sources are the primary reference for their own product specifications, official schematics, maintenance and warranty statements. Retailer claims must be attributed and exact fitment must be corroborated.
- The marketing agent may research, analyze, and draft. Publishing, emailing, spending, account writes, live tracking changes, coupons, and live-site edits need task-specific authorization.
