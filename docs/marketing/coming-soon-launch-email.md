# Coming-soon subscriber launch email

The `dtb-marketing/ComingSoon/LaunchEmail.php` renderer owns the launch announcement content and the administrator-only synthetic preview at **Settings → Launch Email Preview**. Proposed subject: **We’re Live! The New Drywall Toolbox Website Is Now Live**. Preheader: **The new Drywall Toolbox is live. Shop tools, find parts, view schematics, and explore repair services.** The preview uses no customer records and cannot send email.

The template is a ready-to-review design only. It is not connected to subscriber retrieval, a send button, a scheduled task, Action Scheduler, or an automatic site-launch trigger. Do not send until the site is officially live and the launch send is explicitly approved.

Before implementing a one-time sender, establish a durable send identity and duplicate suppression, bounded delivery/retry behavior, and an auditable recipient snapshot drawn only from eligible coming-soon signups. Exclude unsubscribed addresses, preserve recipient-specific unsubscribe links, and keep this audience separate from the WooCommerce account signup coupon campaign. The current coming-soon option list has no launch-send state, and unsubscribe currently removes records from that list; the template alone does not solve campaign dispatch or suppression.

Preview the design in WordPress at **Settings → Launch Email Preview**. The launch content is rendered inside the shared branded email shell, which owns the logo header, typography, responsive frame, and standard footer. The 10% new-account offer is rendered only while the separate signup coupon campaign is active; configure that offer under **Settings → Signup Coupon Campaign**. That settings page does not configure or send the coming-soon subscriber batch.

The launch email uses the supplied `ComingSoon/assets/launch-hero.webp` artwork hosted in the WordPress Media Library, followed by four clickable category badges and the launch information panel from the supplied email mockup. The active icon library is the four supplied PNGs in `drywalltoolbox/logos/email-icons/`: `package.png`, `parts.png`, `schematics.png`, and `repairs.png`. Legacy category/utility icons have been removed; the separate `frontend-*.png` assets remain because the shared footer uses them for social links. Badge links route to Home, Parts, Schematics, and Repair Services. The branded shell supplies the common logo header and global footer (brand logo, social links, account/shop/contact navigation, and copyright). Synthetic preview omits the preview-only unsubscribe placeholder; a real recipient-specific unsubscribe URL is added when the future sender calls the renderer. If the separate 10% offer campaign is inactive, the launch message falls back to a text-led hero without the offer artwork. Verify campaign and route availability again at launch.
