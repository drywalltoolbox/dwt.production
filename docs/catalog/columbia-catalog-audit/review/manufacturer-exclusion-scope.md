# Columbia manufacturer reference: historical exclusions and current disposition

The 1,006-product manufacturer-first reference is a historical selection. Its 48 product exclusions are not accepted as final: all 46 manufacturer-classified `Parts Kit` SKUs and both blister packs (`3WJKBP`, `JKBP`) are reopened as sellable-catalog inclusion candidates. They remain unpublished candidates until exact DTB product-kind/category, supplier cost, and required commercial configuration are verified. The 46 kits are listed in [excluded-manufacturer-part-kits.csv](excluded-manufacturer-part-kits.csv); the two packs are in [excluded-manufacturer-packs.csv](excluded-manufacturer-packs.csv). Complete tool sets have not been excluded solely because they are sets.

The current 1,056-product source accounting also includes two merchandise products with no manufacturer SKU (`Black T-Shirt`, source product `10261614297386`; `Trucker Hat`, source product `10261618098474`). They remain explicitly out of the contractor tool, parts, and service catalog scope. The T-shirt has five size variants and the hat one default variant; none has a SKU.

The complete product/variant reconciliation, field provenance, and pending exception rows are in [current-manufacturer-source-accounting.csv](current-manufacturer-source-accounting.csv), [current-manufacturer-source-provenance.csv](current-manufacturer-source-provenance.csv), and [current-manufacturer-source-exceptions.csv](current-manufacturer-source-exceptions.csv). The files are review evidence, not import files.

The current manufacturer product disposition and category assessments have 1,006 source rows. The manufacturer image manifest contains 1,396 linked local images across 531 products. The working reference has 422 populated weights and 149 rows with all three dimensions. These are field-completeness counts, not verification of physical accuracy or publicly reachable image URLs.

The comprehensive [columbia-products-json-audit.csv](../evidence/manufacturer/columbia-products-json-audit.csv) and earlier 1,054-product shipping audit/change files remain historical evidence. Existing DTB products were not removed because of these historic exclusions. The canonical official catalog has a separately documented, narrow `PTAPER` to `C1PTAPER` identity correction; it is not a complete manufacturer catalog rebuild and is not import-ready.
