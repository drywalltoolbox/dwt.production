# C1PTAPER identity correction

## Evidence and decision

Columbia's [official 2026 catalog](https://www.columbiatools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/CATALOGUE.pdf) and current [official shop listing](https://shopcolumbiatools.com/products/columbia-one-predator-taper) identify the standard Columbia One Predator Taper as `C1PTAPER`. The older [Columbia product page](https://www.columbiatools.com/columbia-tools/automatic-tapers/columbia-one-predator-taper/) still uses `PTAPER`. Per the explicit catalog instruction, the existing DTB product identity is retained and its current catalog identity is corrected to `C1PTAPER`; no second taper product was created.

## Canonical change

The single canonical product row changed from `PTAPER` to `C1PTAPER`. The required aligned fields `Meta: _dtb_manufacturer_sku`, `Meta: schema_mpn`, and `Meta: _dtb_mpn` now use `C1PTAPER`, as required by `scripts/catalog/official_catalog_schema.py`. The customer-facing Part Number specification and SEO SKU reference were updated. Seventy-six exact compatibility references on Columbia-owned rows changed to `C1PTAPER`. The active schematic map recognizes `C1PTAPER` and retains `PTAPER` as a legacy lookup alias.

The row's separate `model` value remains `PTAPER`; price, cost, stock, status, visibility, category, parentage, permalink, media, and Veeqo mapping are unchanged. The frontend schematic mapping and backend schematic map now recognize `C1PTAPER` while preserving `PTAPER` as an alias. The official catalog’s six other modular-family SKUs—`C1PBODY`, `C1PHEAD`, `C1SPTAPER`, `C1SPBODY`, `C1TBPTAPER`, and `C1TBPBODY`—are explicitly classified as separate new-product candidates, pending current cost, taxonomy, and commerce configuration. The repair-service model index remains on the live `PTAPER` identifier pending the production SKU cutover. Existing hashed compatibility projection release batches also retain their historical `PTAPER` values and were left unchanged; their pinned catalog SHA does not match the current canonical catalog, so they are not valid for replay. The canonical CSV retains 1,133 rows and its 150-column schema. Its SHA-256 changed from `2c3af76f6bdcc9f1863948111f11d5f93791eee29865f7524b9bd770c5f0f41d` to `ab8b6dbf1756f9df7f389bdd792322de83b3091cab23abe893d7fd738114aa06`.

The exact pre-change catalog is preserved at [rollback snapshot](../archive/rollback-snapshots/dtb_official_catalog-before-ptaper-c1ptaper-20261111.csv) and `products/launch/official/dtb_official_catalog.csv.bak`. The apply receipt is [ptaper-c1ptaper-catalog-migration.json](ptaper-c1ptaper-catalog-migration.json). The prior sibling backup is separately archived at `../archive/rollback-snapshots/dtb_official_catalog-preexisting-sibling-bak-20261111.csv`.

It remains a published simple product in Automatic Tapers with current price `$1,838.55`, regular price `$2,022.41`, COGS `$1,458.91`, and stock quantity 10. Prices, costs, stock, fulfillment, media, and Veeqo records were not changed. No deployment, commit, or push occurred. The full Columbia rebuild remains incomplete and is not import-ready. Current source accounting records 1,056 source products and 1,060 variants: 435 exact existing matches, 529 new-product candidates, 19 possible matches, 48 reopened kit/pack candidates, 15 resolved alternate-identifier matches pending commercial review, one resolved alternate identity pending taxonomy and commercial review, three remaining identifier conflicts, one catalog SKU collision, two duplicate-source SKU rows, and two excluded merchandise products; the explicit `PTAPER` to `C1PTAPER` correction remains separately counted.
