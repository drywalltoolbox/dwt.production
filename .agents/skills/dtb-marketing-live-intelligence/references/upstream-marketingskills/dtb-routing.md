# DTB routing for the upstream marketing corpus

The upstream index holds all 52 source methods. Select at most three for a request and open only those full files. The DTB method and live evidence remain authoritative. These groupings are routing suggestions, not endorsements or required bundles.

| DTB task | Upstream source methods | DTB-specific adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning, audience, strategy, planning | `product-marketing`, `customer-research`, `marketing-plan`, `marketing-ideas`, `marketing-council`, `marketing-psychology` | Validate with contractor, catalog, service, and business evidence; distinguish evidence from hypotheses. Marketing council is a perspective exercise, not independent real experts. |
| Competitive and market research | `competitor-profiling`, `competitors`, `customer-research`, `marketing-ideas` | Date observations; distinguish manufacturer, distributor, marketplace, and service competitor; don't infer sales or ad spend from public pages. |
| Product/category SEO and discovery | `seo-audit`, `ai-seo`, `programmatic-seo`, `schema`, `site-architecture`, `link-building`, `aso` | Use `.agents/skills/dtb-seo`; route content to real public routes and authoritative catalog data. Never fabricate attributes or index private/session routes. Treat search/LLM tactics as hypotheses subject to current platform guidance. |
| Content strategy and editorial | `content-strategy`, `copywriting`, `copy-editing`, `marketing-ideas`, `free-tools` | Ground briefs in contractor questions, exact product identity, supportable facts, and useful destinations; don't mass-generate thin taxonomy pages. |
| CRO, onboarding, forms, experiments | `cro`, `signup`, `onboarding`, `popups`, `paywalls`, `ab-testing` | Use storefront and repair service journeys; preserve native WooCommerce checkout/provider ownership. Require observed friction, an instrumented hypothesis, guardrails, and a stop rule. SaaS signup/paywall patterns may not fit ecommerce. |
| Analytics and conversion tracking | `analytics`, `attribution`, `conversion-tracking` | Use active DTB tracking, consent, and tag ownership. A sample script or successful local preview is not proof of live measurement. Publishing tags or generating test conversions requires explicit authorization. |
| Paid media and creative | `ads`, `ad-creative`, `image` | Use verified product images, claims, margin/fulfillment constraints, inventory authority, channel policy, and approved spend. Drafts do not authorize account writes or budget changes. |
| Lifecycle, retention, referrals | `emails`, `sms`, `churn-prevention`, `referrals`, `community-marketing` | Use consented customer journeys and actual policies. Never send messages, alter flows, issue offers, or contact customers without task-specific authorization. SaaS churn and referral assumptions need ecommerce evidence. |
| Launch, partnerships, PR, events | `launch`, `co-marketing`, `public-relations`, `events`, `influencer-marketing`, `directory-submissions` | Check real product/repair readiness, supply, service capacity, approved claims, partner terms, and release timing. Outreach, submissions, or public posts remain drafts until authorized. |
| Lead generation and sales operations | `prospecting`, `cold-email`, `revops`, `sales-enablement` | Use only a justified, lawful B2B motion; protect personal data, provenance, suppression, and platform terms. No scraping, enrichment, or outreach merely because a skill describes it. |
| Product-led growth and monetization | `offers`, `pricing`, `lead-magnets`, `marketing-loops`, `directory-submissions` | Adapt SaaS activation, freemium, ARR, and subscription assumptions to physical product economics, gross margin, replacement cycles, and fulfillment. Price authority remains WooCommerce. |
| Video and social production | `social`, `video`, `image`, `ad-creative` | Match platform format while keeping product identity, physical geometry, compatibility, and claims accurate. Generated visuals must not misrepresent the actual product. |

All remaining slugs are directly listed in `index.json`. If no method maps cleanly to the task, use the DTB method without forcing an upstream pattern.
